Generative AI tools, including large language models (LLMs), are forbidden from
contributing in any way to Sopel or its subprojects.

Please don't submit AI-generated comments, code, or other content in this
project. We understand that some people feel AI is useful for them—but we want
to connect with *you*, not your computer. We want our code to be art. We want to
interact with real humans.

Any pull request created in this repo will ask you to confirm that you are the
author, and that you license your changes under the Eiffel Forum License 2.
